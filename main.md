 ## UNDERSTANDING THE BENEFITS OF DIVINE WISDOM – PART 2

 ### 1\. DIVINE WISDOM PROVOKES CREATIVITY

 **Proverbs 8:12; Daniel 1:17, 20**

 > **Proverbs 8:12:** “I wisdom dwell with prudence, and find out knowledge of witty inventions.”

 Wisdom is the fountain of creative ideas and innovative solutions. Where divine wisdom is at work, limitations become opportunities for creative possibilities.

 God does not only give His people information; He gives them **ideas, insight, ingenuity and inventive capacity**.

 Daniel and his companions demonstrated this dimension of wisdom:

 > **Daniel 1:17:** “God gave them knowledge and skill in all learning and wisdom…”

 And when they were examined by the king:

 > **Daniel 1:20:** “…in all matters of wisdom and understanding… he found them ten times better than all the magicians and astrologers…”

 ### What divine wisdom does creatively:

 - It gives access to **witty inventions**.
- It produces solutions where others see problems.
- It gives unusual understanding and insight.
- It causes believers to excel in their fields.
- It makes God's people relevant in their generation.
- It distinguishes a person above conventional human capacity.

 **Daniel did not compete with Babylon by copying Babylon; he excelled by the wisdom that came from God.**

 Therefore, divine wisdom makes a believer a solution provider.

 ### 2\. DIVINE WISDOM COMMANDS EXPLOITS

 **Psalm 104:24; Matthew 13:54**

 > **Psalm 104:24:** “O Lord, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all…”

 Everything God created reflects His wisdom. The vastness, order, diversity and functionality of creation demonstrate that wisdom is a force for extraordinary accomplishment.

 Jesus also demonstrated the connection between wisdom and mighty works:

 > **Matthew 13:54:** “…Whence hath this man this wisdom, and these mighty works?”

 Notice that they connected **wisdom** with **mighty works**.

 This establishes that divine wisdom does not merely make a person knowledgeable; it empowers the person to produce results.

 ### What this means:

 - Wisdom turns knowledge into practical results.
- Wisdom shows you **what to do, how to do it and when to do it**.
- Wisdom makes exploits possible.
- Wisdom brings order to resources and opportunities.
- Wisdom causes ordinary resources to produce extraordinary results.

 The question is not merely, **“What do I have?”**

 The question is, **“What can divine wisdom make out of what I have?”**

 The five loaves and two fishes were insufficient in the natural, but divine wisdom knew what to do with them.

 Therefore, **what is inadequate in the hands of a man without wisdom can become sufficient in the hands of a man operating by divine wisdom.**

 ## CONCLUSION

 Divine wisdom is a major asset for kingdom advancement and personal distinction.

 It:

 1. **Provokes creativity** — Proverbs 8:12.
2. **Commands exploits** — Psalm 104:24; Matthew 13:54.

 As we engage the wisdom of God, we receive grace for **creative ideas, supernatural insight, uncommon solutions and outstanding results.**
